# Text labels used in the graphical abstract

Every text string that appears on the figure, grouped by location. "Full" = the
non-abbreviated meaning; abbreviations are always spelled out immediately next to
their first (and only) use, so none appear unexplained.

## Present in both the full and clean versions

**Management loop**
- ADAPTIVE MANAGEMENT FEEDBACK
- AVOID · MINIMIZE · RESTORE · MONITOR (the four waypoints, in loop order)

**Pressures panel**
- PRESSURES
- anthropogenic + climatic drivers
- Dams, flow regulation
- Water diversion, pumping
- Drainage, reclamation
- Land-use change
- Climate: rainfall extremes, sea-level rise

**Wetland cross-section — zone labels**
- HYDROLOGY
- HABITAT
- TROPHIC LINKS
- ECOSYSTEM FUNCTIONING

**Assessment gauges** (acronym, spelled out immediately beneath it)
- HVI — hydroperiod variability
- HTR — habitat turnover
- CI — functional connectivity
- TRP — trophic redundancy
- EFS — eco-hydrological feedback strength

**Ecological consequences panel**
- ECOLOGICAL CONSEQUENCES
- Habitat simplification, loss of ecotones
- Reduced trophic redundancy
- Resource–consumer timing mismatch
- Waterbird decline across flyways
- Reduced C storage, nutrient retention, flood-water buffering

## Full version only (removed in the clean/submission variant)

- HHTE — a process-based framework for wetland assessment *(title kicker)*
- Hydrology → Habitat → Trophic organization → Ecosystem functioning *(subtitle)*
- acts on pressures *(annotation on the management arrow)*
- natural hydroperiod (reference) *(dashed water-level annotation)*
- hydroperiod shift *(annotation at the reference/altered water-level gap)*
- ECOSYSTEM FUNCTIONING — carbon storage · nutrient retention · flood regulation
  *(caption under the soil band)*
- red tick = management-trigger threshold *(gauge legend)*
- "Process-based indicators (HVI · HTR · CI · TRP · EFS) are compared with
  management triggers to activate an avoid – minimize – restore – monitor
  response." *(bottom caption)*

## Non-text pictograms (no label, meaning given in the rationale)
- Soil-band icons: **C** (carbon), **N** (nutrients), a stylised wave (flood
  regulation)
- Pressures panel icons: dam, diversion valve, drainage lines, land-use tile,
  sun/climate burst
- Consequences panel icons: fragmented habitat tiles, two bird silhouettes (one
  faded, indicating decline), clock (timing mismatch), downward function arrow
